Half-hourly electricity analyser

Load one Excel or CSV file of half-hourly readings per meter, ideally 12 months for each site on your estate. The analyser checks the data, then shows base load, day and night use, out-of-hours use in clinics and admin buildings, peaks against your agreed supply capacity, headroom for heat pumps, how much solar each site would use, and unusual days, with PDF and Excel reports.

It runs entirely in your browser: your meter data is never uploaded. It needs JavaScript and a recent desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge.
