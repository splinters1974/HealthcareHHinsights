NHS estates energy: facts and figures

The numbers estates teams, boards and suppliers ask for most, in one place: the size of the estate, net zero dates, carbon factors, funding and the biggest single savings. Each one is sourced and dated.

By Paul Johnston, Managing Director, Kinetic Energy Consulting · Last updated 22 November 2026

NHS trusts in England spent about £14.4 billion running their estates in 2025/26 and used around 11 billion kWh of energy.1 The NHS in England aims for net zero by 2040 for the emissions it controls, NHS Scotland by 2040 with renewable heat in all NHS-owned buildings by 2038, and NHS Wales has interim targets of a 34% cut by 2030 and no fossil CHP after 2035.4,6,8,9 The grid electricity carbon factor for 2026 reporting is about 0.13 kg CO₂e per kWh.11

Independent guidance. Hospital Energy Insights is not part of, or endorsed by, the NHS, NHS England, the Scottish Government or the Welsh Government. We summarise their published policy and link to it. Always check the source and your own organisation's policies before relying on it.

Questions people ask

How much energy does the NHS use?

NHS trusts in England used around 11 billion kWh of energy in a year, according to the Estates Returns Information Collection, and spent about £14.4 billion running their estates in 2025/26.

When does the NHS have to reach net zero?

In England, 2040 for emissions the NHS controls directly and 2045 including the supply chain. NHS Scotland aims for 2040, with renewable heat in all NHS-owned buildings by 2038. NHS Wales has interim targets of 16% by 2025 and 34% by 2030.

What is the grid carbon factor for 2026?

About 0.13 kg CO2e per kWh in the UK government conversion factors for 2026 reporting, down from 0.177 for 2025.

How much energy does theatre ventilation setback save?

NHS England estimates 132 to 286 MWh per operating theatre each year, across about 3,300 theatres in England.
