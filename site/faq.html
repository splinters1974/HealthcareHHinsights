FAQ

Questions from estates teams

If yours isn't here, ask us.

Hospital Energy Insights gives NHS and independent healthcare estates a free half-hourly electricity analyser, a solar calculator and sourced guides to decarbonisation. You load one Excel or CSV file per meter, ideally a year of readings; the analysis happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

About the tools

Who is Hospital Energy Insights for?

Estates, facilities, energy and sustainability teams in NHS trusts, health boards, integrated care boards, ambulance trusts and independent hospital groups in England, Scotland and Wales. Capital planning and finance teams use the reports too.

Is it an NHS service?

No. It is independent, run by Kinetic Energy Consulting, and not part of or endorsed by the NHS. The guides summarise NHS England, Scottish Government and Welsh Government policy and link to the originals.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no fee, no account and no limit on sites. If you want help taking the results further, get in touch, but there is no obligation. A user guide (PDF) is free too.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop, and one half-hourly file per meter. Nothing to install. The charts need a big screen, so it is not built for phones.

Can I keep my work and come back to it?

Yes. Save a project file to your computer. It holds the readings and every setting, so store it as you would the original meter data.

Hospitals and other healthcare sites

Does it work on a hospital that never closes?

Yes. New sites start on the 24/7 hospital preset, which has no closed hours and uses 01:00 to 05:00 as a low-activity window. The analysis concentrates on base load and its monthly trend, peaks against capacity, unusual days and solar. Switch health centres, outpatient blocks and offices to the clinic or office preset.

We have two incoming supplies. How do we load them?

Load one file per meter and group them into one site in the settings, so peaks are checked against capacity on their combined demand.

Our hospital runs a CHP engine. Can we still use it?

Yes, but the import meter only shows what the grid supplies while the engine runs. Load the CHP generation meter as its own file to see total demand, and look at engine-off days before assuming there is headroom. See moving away from CHP.

Can it find a theatre ventilation setback that has stopped working?

Often, yes. A failed setback shows as a step up in base load by month and darker nights and weekends on the heatmap. The worked example shows one.

Is it any use for a PFI hospital?

Yes, if you can get the meter data. On many PFI sites the project company or its facilities management provider holds it, so ask them. See getting half-hourly data.

Can it help with ERIC or EFPMS returns?

It does not fill in the returns, but it gives you the annual electricity figure for each meter and explains the patterns behind it. See ERIC, EFPMS and benchmarking.

Data and information governance

Is any data uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your web browser and the files are read on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and once the page has loaded you can work offline. The privacy notice has the detail.

Will our IG team approve it?

Half-hourly electricity data records energy, not people, so it holds no patient information, and the files never leave your computer. Share the privacy notice if your IG team wants to review it.

What happens to gaps and errors in our files?

The analyser checks before it reports. Missing readings are left out, never counted as zero; zero days, clock-change days and duplicates are held back for you to decide on.

What reaches the solar calculator?

Only a summary: annual use, the share of solar the site would use at a dozen sizes, and the postcode if you entered one. It travels after the # in the web address, which browsers never send to a server.

Using the results

Are the costs savings?

No. They are what a pattern costs now at your rates. A saving depends on what you change, and should be confirmed from the meter data afterwards. See proving a saving worked.

How far can we trust a finding?

Each one has an impact rating and a separate confidence rating, with the evidence and calculation behind it. Confirm the calendar for each building and confidence goes up.

Can it size a heat pump?

It answers the electrical half: whether the supply can take the heat pump's load through the heating months. Sizing the heat side needs heat data and a designer. See supply capacity and headroom.

Does it analyse gas?

Not yet; it reads electricity data. Gas and heat analysis is planned. Meanwhile, the heat decarbonisation guide covers what to look at.

How do we share results with the board or a funder?

Download a PDF or Excel report for a site or the whole estate, or create a password-protected dashboard containing only the sites you choose.
