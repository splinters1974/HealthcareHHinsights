FAQ

Questions and answers

If your question isn't answered here, ask us.

Healthcare HH Insights is a free half-hourly electricity analyser and solar calculator for NHS and independent healthcare estates across the UK, with guides to decarbonising the estate. The analyser runs in your web browser, so your meter files are never uploaded. You need one Excel or CSV file per meter, ideally covering 12 months.

Using the analyser

Who is it for?

Estates, facilities, energy and sustainability teams in NHS trusts, health boards, integrated care boards, ambulance trusts and independent hospitals across England, Scotland and Wales. It works for any building with a half-hourly meter, but the settings, guides and examples are written for healthcare.

Is this an NHS tool?

No. Healthcare HH Insights is independent and is not part of, or endorsed by, the NHS. It is run by Kinetic Energy Consulting. Our guides summarise and link to NHS, Scottish Government and Welsh Government policy.

Will it work for a 24-hour hospital?

Yes. Use the hospital preset, which has no closed hours. The analysis then focuses on base load, day and night use, peaks against capacity and lasting changes. Give clinics and offices on the site their real opening hours.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge, no account and no limit on the number of sites. If you'd like help acting on the results you can contact us, but there's no obligation.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a recent computer. It isn't designed for phones, because the charts need a larger screen.

How many sites can I load?

As many as your computer can handle. Each meter file becomes a site, and the analyser ranks them so you know which to look at first.

Is there a user guide?

Yes. Download the user guide (PDF). It takes you through getting your data, loading it, setting opening hours, reading the results, downloading reports and creating customer dashboards.

Can I save my work?

Yes. Save a project file to your computer and open it again later. It contains the readings and all your settings, so store it as carefully as the original data.

Will our information governance team approve it?

The analysis runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded, so no meter data leaves your computer, and half-hourly electricity data contains no patient information. Share the privacy notice with your IG team if they want the details.

How does the solar calculator get my data?

On a site's Solar tab, Size solar for this site opens the solar calculator with a summary: the site's annual use, the share of solar output it would use at a dozen system sizes, and the postcode if you entered one. They travel after the # in the web address, which browsers do not send to any server. The readings stay on your computer.

Can I share results with a client or colleague?

Yes. You can download a PDF report for one site or your whole portfolio, export to Excel, or create a customer dashboard. That's a password-protected copy of the analyser containing only their sites.

Your data

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The analysis runs entirely in your web browser. Your meter files are read on your own computer and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Once the page has loaded you can even disconnect from the internet and keep working. Our privacy notice has the details.

What data do I need?

Half-hourly electricity readings for each meter, ideally covering 12 months or more. Here is how to get them.

What if my file has gaps or errors?

The analyser finds them before showing any figures. Missing readings are left out rather than counted as zero, and days with zero readings or clock changes are flagged for you to decide on.

The results

Are the costs savings?

No. They show what a pattern costs now at the rates you entered. Any saving depends on what you change. The savings estimates are clearly labelled as estimates.

How accurate is it?

It's as accurate as the data and opening hours you give it. Each finding shows a confidence rating and the evidence behind it, and each figure is labelled as measured or estimated.

Can it size heat pumps?

Not the heat side: that needs heat data and a designer. It does answer the electrical question first, by testing a heat pump's electrical load against each supply's winter peaks and agreed capacity. See electrical capacity.

Does it analyse gas?

Not yet. It reads electricity meter data. Gas and heat analysis is planned. Our heat decarbonisation guide covers what to look at in the meantime.

Can it tell me which equipment is wasting energy?

No. Data from the main meter shows when electricity is used, but not what is using it. The findings tell you when and where to look, and a visit to the building finds the cause.
