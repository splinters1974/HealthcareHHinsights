You can use Hospital Energy Insights free of charge to analyse your own organisation's electricity data. If you use it to produce reports, dashboards or advice for other organisations, as part of paid work or to win it, you need a licence from us.

Who we are

Hospital Energy Insights (hospitalenergy.co.uk) is run by Kinetic Energy Consulting. See about us. These terms apply to the website, the analyser and everything you download from it.

Free use

Any organisation can use the analyser, the guides and the downloads free for its own sites and buildings. That includes energy and estates managers, finance teams and facilities staff analysing their own organisation's data, and sharing the reports, spreadsheets and customer editions within their organisation and with its own board, landlord or auditors.

Work for clients needs a licence

If you are a consultant, broker, supplier, installer or anyone else using the analyser to produce reports, dashboards, customer editions or advice for another organisation, as part of paid work or to win it, you need a licence. That includes handing a customer edition or a PDF from the analyser to a client. Contact us about a licence.

Keep our name on what you download

Every PDF page, spreadsheet, chart image and customer edition made with the analyser is marked "Made with Hospital Energy Insights" with this website's address. Do not remove, hide or change that marking, or present the output as made by another tool, unless your licence allows it.

Copying the site

Do not copy the analyser or this website, in whole or in part, to offer a similar service, and do not resell access to it.

Estimates, not advice

Results are estimates from the data you load and the settings you enter. Costs are energy cost at the rates entered, not guaranteed savings, and the guides are general information, not financial, tax or legal advice. Check anything important before you rely on it. We are not responsible for decisions made on the results, but nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

Your data

Your meter data stays on your computer. The privacy notice explains the few things we do receive, such as contact form enquiries and the details asked for when you create a customer edition.

Changes and law

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
