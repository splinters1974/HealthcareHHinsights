Net zero targets for healthcare across the UK

The NHS in England, NHS Scotland and NHS Wales each have their own targets and dates. Here they are side by side, with what each means for the estate.

By Paul Johnston, Managing Director, Kinetic Energy Consulting · Last updated 22 November 2026

The NHS in England aims for net zero by 2040 for the emissions it controls directly, and 2045 for the emissions it can influence. NHS Scotland aims for net zero by 2040, with all NHS-owned buildings using renewable heat by 2038. NHS Wales is working to a 34% reduction by 2030 as part of a Welsh public sector net zero ambition, with all fossil-fuelled CHP to be decommissioned by 2035.

Independent guidance. Hospital Energy Insights is not part of, or endorsed by, the NHS, NHS England, the Scottish Government or the Welsh Government. We summarise their published policy and link to it. Always check the source and your own organisation's policies before relying on it.

England

The two targets were set in 2020 against a 1990 baseline. The NHS Carbon Footprint covers emissions the NHS controls directly, including the energy used in its buildings. The Carbon Footprint Plus adds the supply chain, medicines and other emissions the NHS can influence.1 NHS England reports that direct emissions have fallen by over 68% since 1990, and that it remains on track for the interim 80% target by 2032.2

For estates teams, this shows up in three places: the trust's Green Plan, the heat decarbonisation plan behind any capital bid, and the NHS Net Zero Building Standard for new buildings and major upgrades.3

Scotland

NHS Scotland brought its net zero date forward from 2045 to 2040. Its strategy commits to cutting emissions from buildings by at least 75% against 1990, using renewable heating systems in all NHS-owned buildings by 2038, and making the whole estate net zero by 2040 or earlier.4 New facilities must either use renewable heat from the start or have a clear plan to do so by 2038. The 2025 annual report describes power purchase agreements as a core part of the strategy, because renewable electricity cuts the cost of electrified heat and transport.5

Wales

NHS organisations in Wales work to the NHS Wales Decarbonisation Strategic Delivery Plan, now updated for 2025 to 2030. It sets interim reductions of 16% by 2025 and 34% by 2030.7 The buildings section is specific about plant: no further fossil-fuelled CHP is to be installed, existing CHP should run at maximum efficiency until life expired, decommissioning is preferred over major refurbishment such as engine replacement, and all fossil CHP is to be decommissioned by 2035. It also asks for seasonal reviews of building controls such as BMS settings, timers and thermostats.6

Suppliers and private providers

Since April 2024, NHS England has required a carbon reduction plan for all new procurements, and every NHS procurement since April 2022 has carried at least a 10% weighting for net zero and social value.8 From 1 April 2027, a stricter carbon reduction plan standard applies to larger contracts and new frameworks.9 That matters to independent hospitals delivering NHS work, and to every contractor quoting for estate projects.

Independent healthcare providers have their own sector commitment, led by the Independent Healthcare Providers Network, to reach net zero by 2035.10 See private and independent hospitals.

What this means for your data

Questions people ask

When does the NHS in England have to reach net zero?

By 2040 for the emissions it controls directly (the NHS Carbon Footprint), with an 80% cut by 2028 to 2032, and by 2045 for the emissions it can influence (the Carbon Footprint Plus).

What is NHS Scotland's target for heating?

All NHS-owned buildings are to use renewable heating systems by 2038, with the whole estate net zero by 2040 or earlier.

Can NHS Wales install new gas CHP?

No. The NHS Wales decarbonisation plan says no further fossil-fuelled CHP will be installed, existing units should run until life expired, and all fossil CHP should be decommissioned by 2035. Renewable CHP is still supported.
