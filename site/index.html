One journey, from meter data to a fundable project

Most estates already have half-hourly data: suppliers record it for billing. It is the evidence behind almost every decarbonisation decision, so the journey starts there.

1

Upload

Drag in one file per meter for every site. The analyser checks the readings, flags gaps and clock changes, and builds each site's year.

2

Understand

See base load, day and night use, out-of-hours use in clinics and offices, unusual days and lasting changes, priced at your rates.

3

Check headroom

Compare each supply's peaks with its agreed capacity, and test the load from heat pumps, EV chargers or switching off CHP.

4

Size solar

The Solar tab measures how much output each site would use, then opens the solar calculator with your figures to map roofs and car parks.

5

Report

Download PDF and Excel reports for each site and the whole estate, ready for a Green Plan update, a capital bid or a funding application.

The challenge estates teams face

Net zero dates are fixed, the money has changed shape, and buildings that never close have to keep running while their heat is replaced. Read the full picture.

Net zero by 2040

NHS England aims for net zero by 2040 for the emissions it controls, NHS Scotland by 2040 with renewable heat by 2038, and NHS Wales has set 34% by 2030. Targets compared.

Heat is the big one

Gas boilers and CHP are usually the largest source of carbon. Replacing them means heat pumps, lower flow temperatures and new answers for steam and hot water. Heat decarbonisation.

CHP no longer adds up on carbon

With the grid factor at about 0.13 kg CO₂e per kWh, a typical gas CHP engine now emits more than grid electricity and a boiler. Moving away from CHP.

The grid connection

Heat pumps add winter load, and removing CHP adds its full output to grid import. Headroom decides what is possible, and when. Electrical capacity.

Funding has changed

No new PSDS money is being committed. Great British Energy, devolved schemes, PPAs and energy performance contracts fill some of the gap. Funding routes.

Waste comes first

Theatre ventilation, BMS schedules, lighting and steam losses are usually the cheapest carbon to remove, and make everything after smaller. Energy efficiency.

Built for NHS information governance

The analyser does all its work inside your web browser. Meter files are read on your own computer and are never sent to us or anyone else. There is no patient data involved, no account and no tracking.

Nothing uploaded

No server is involved in the analysis. Once the page has loaded, you can disconnect from the internet and carry on working.

No account

No sign-up, no email address needed to analyse, and no tracking cookies.

You keep the results

Reports, spreadsheets and project files are saved to your computer. Save a project to come back to it later.

Who it's for

Estates, facilities and energy teams

Built for the people who run healthcare buildings and have to make the numbers work: estates and facilities managers, energy and sustainability managers, capital planning teams and finance. In the NHS and in independent hospital groups.

  • Community and mental health sites: inpatient units beside clinics with set hours
