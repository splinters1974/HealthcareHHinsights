About Hospital Energy Insights

An independent site for healthcare estates teams, run by Kinetic Energy Consulting.

Hospital Energy Insights gives NHS and independent healthcare estates three free things: a half-hourly electricity analyser that runs in the browser, a solar calculator fed by the measured profile, and guides to decarbonising the estate, each sourced and dated. It's run by Kinetic Energy Consulting; Paul Johnston, its Managing Director, writes and reviews the guides. It is independent and not part of, or endorsed by, the NHS.

Paul Johnston

Managing Director, Kinetic Energy Consulting

Paul's career has been in the UK energy market, at some of the country's largest suppliers and with private-equity-backed start-ups building new energy businesses.

The suppliers showed him how much detail the industry already records about when and how organisations use electricity. The start-ups showed him what happens when that detail reaches someone who can act on it. Hospitals are where the gap costs most: sites that never close, net zero dates that don't move, and connections that take years to upgrade.

Why this site exists

Estates teams are asked to decarbonise buildings they can't close, often with less capital than the plan needs and grid connections that are already near their limit. The evidence for most of those decisions is already sitting with the electricity supplier as half-hourly data, recorded for billing. It rarely reaches the estates, energy or capital planning teams who need it.

So the site starts there. Load the data, see what each building is doing, check the headroom for heat pumps, measure how much solar each site would use, and take a report to the board or a funder.

Why it's free, and how we're paid

The tools and guides cost nothing and need no sign-up. Kinetic Energy Consulting is paid when an organisation asks for help beyond them, for example with heat pump feasibility, solar design, funding applications or procurement. Using the site never commits you to that. Because the analysis runs in your browser, we only see your data if you choose to send it.

How we keep the guides accurate

  • Primary sources. Figures cite NHS England, the Scottish and Welsh Governments, Ofgem, GOV.UK or the organisation concerned, with the date we checked.
  • A review cycle. Funding and policy pages are reviewed every six months and straight away when a scheme opens or closes. Prices and rates are reviewed every April.
  • Tested examples. The worked example hospital is made up, and says so, but every figure on its page is checked against the analyser by automated tests.
  • Costs, not promises. We show what a pattern costs now, not a guaranteed saving, and say where findings overlap.
  • Corrections. If something is wrong or out of date, tell us and we'll fix it and update the date.
